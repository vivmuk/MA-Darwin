Structure

The deck follows the expected 8-slide M2M arc (title → disease context → unmet need → evidence → safety → unknowns → references → backup). Slide order is sound, but the evidence slide (4) tries to carry the full study design, sample size, and three endpoints in one view — it should be split. There is no standalone executive-summary slide, so a reader skimming the first 20 seconds gets no synthesis.

Content

Claims on slides 2–4 are traceable to the source PDF. The safety slide (5) is appropriately weighted but uses a dense paragraph where a structured table (event, arm, rate, difference) would read faster. Confidence intervals are stated inconsistently — present on the primary endpoint, missing on the secondary. The "what remains unknown" slide (6) is currently a single sentence and under-developed relative to its importance in scientific exchange.

Quality & compliance

Tone is non-promotional and the DRAFT marking is retained on the title slide. References list only identifiers that appear in the source. Visual density is the main quality risk: 4 of 8 slides exceed ~40 words of body copy, which pushes them past a comfortable presenter-support threshold.

Analysis & Suggested Improvements

Where your feedback and the AI evaluation agreed

  • Both flagged the evidence slide (4) as overloaded — you asked for "a clearer confidence interval," the AI flagged the same slide for carrying too many endpoints at once.
  • Both rated the safety framing positively; no change needed to its prominence or placement.

Where they diverged

  • You did not mention the missing executive-summary slide; the AI evaluation raised it as a priority gap.
  • The AI called slides 2–3 "dense paragraphs"; your notes did not comment on prose density outside slide 4.

Suggested changes to the PDF → PowerPoint skill

  1. structure

    Add an executive-summary slide as slide 2

    Insert a synthesis slide (3–4 bullets: population, key finding with effect size, key safety signal, open question) immediately after the title. Renumber the existing 8-slide outline to 9.

  2. density

    Cap body copy at ~40 words / 5 bullets per slide

    When source content for a slide exceeds the cap, split into two slides rather than shrinking type. Prefer bullet points over multi-sentence paragraphs on slides 2–4.

  3. evidence

    Split the evidence slide into "design" and "results"

    One slide for study design + N + population; a second for outcomes. Require a confidence interval on every reported estimate, primary and secondary — flag any estimate missing a CI in the draft.

  4. safety

    Render safety data as a table, not a paragraph

    Columns: adverse event, arm, rate, between-arm difference. Keep the oxblood accent reserved for the safety slide only.

  5. completeness

    Expand "what remains unknown" to 3+ points

    Require at least three distinct evidence gaps (e.g. long-term data, subgroup effects, comparator scope) rather than a single summary sentence.
