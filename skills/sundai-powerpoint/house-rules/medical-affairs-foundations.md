## Seeded examples

These are illustrative. Replace them with your own SOP requirements.

- Route all potential adverse events to `[your PV mailbox]` within 24 hours of
  detection, and record the detection time — our SOP clock starts at detection,
  not at forwarding.
- Our organisation does not permit any discussion of unapproved uses in field
  settings. All such requests route to Medical Information without exception,
  including when the requester insists the question is general.
- State the jurisdiction explicitly in every deliverable. We operate in markets
  with materially different approval status and our reviewers have been burned
  by documents that assumed US labelling.
