# House rules — `medical-slide-deck`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/medical-slide-deck/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Use the corporate medical template at `[path]`. Decks in any other template
  are returned unreviewed.
- Every data slide carries the reference in the footer, not on a references
  slide at the end. Reviewers check slide by slide.
- Slide titles state findings, not conclusions. "ORR 63% (single-arm, n=165)",
  not "substantial activity".
