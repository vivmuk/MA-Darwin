# House rules — `evidence-appraisal`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/evidence-appraisal/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Do not cite congress abstracts in any externally facing document. Internal use
  only, and always labelled. Abstract results change too often for us to stand
  behind them externally.
- Any hazard ratio quoted must carry its confidence interval and the event
  count. A point estimate from few events has misled us before.
- Never quote a median PFS without stating the assessment schedule. Our TA has
  trials ranging from 6-weekly to 12-weekly imaging and the medians are not
  comparable.
