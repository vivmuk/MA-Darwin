# Style rules (taste / preference)

- Maximum 6 bullets per slide; maximum 12 words per bullet unless a ledger
  verbatim snippet is longer and must be quoted intact.
- Body text minimum 14pt (see `config/thresholds.yaml`).
- At most 2 font families and 5 colours in the deck.
- Prefer editable OOXML charts over rasterized chart images.
- Absolute-risk callouts when percent changes are large on low baselines.
- Keep one primary takeaway per slide; avoid competing focal points.
